Books banned by the thousands across U.S. school districts

By Nicole Ramos

April 20, 2026

Between 2024-2025, there were over 6,700 book bans or restrictions in school districts from 21 states and educational facilities run by the Department of Defense, according to a report published by PEN America on Oct. 1, 2025. Banning proponents argue that certain material may be harmful to young readers, while opponents argue banning certain titles stifles academic freedom and is often used as a political tactic. PEN America, who tracks and publishes nation-wide banning data annually, said the United States faces a "contemporary book banning crisis."
